# Overgear design constitution

Version 1.0. 3 product rules. Generated from `overgear/constitution/rules.js`; do not edit this file directly.

These rules apply only to Overgear. Also follow the shared [baseui constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md) and its agent protocol. They are not defaults for other products.

## For agents

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before changing the store. Add every new Overgear-specific rule to `overgear/constitution/rules.js`, with a stable `OG`-prefixed id. Add its checks and tests under `overgear/` too. Run `npm run constitution` after editing rules.

Use the Overgear banner when reporting a violation; it resolves both shared and local rule ids:

```jsx
import { ConstitutionBanner } from 'baseui-kit/overgear';

<ConstitutionBanner violations={[{ id: 'OG3', note: 'The Catalog button opens a product listing' }]} />
```

`audit` on the banner checks shared automatic rules. OG1 and OG3 require review; the whole-row part of OG2 is checked by `npm run check:overgear:spacing -- http://localhost:8000`.

## Store rules

### OG1 Screens of a bottom bar, empty states (should)

- **Do:** On a phone the root screen of each tab of the bottom bar has no site header and no footer: the bar is the navigation. A login prompt or an empty screen with nothing else to show takes the whole screen and stands in the middle. Put a list of the few settings a visitor can change (currency, language, support) right under the login button
- **Don't:** repeat the site header and footer on the tab screens, show recommendations on an empty cart, or open a login prompt in the middle of a screen that is otherwise empty
- **Why:** A tab is a place of its own; an empty state is for one next step
- **Source:** Mobile patterns: tab bar; Material and HIG navigation; needs a review
- **Requests that lead here:** remove the header; remove the footer; stretch the block; remove the recommendations

### OG2 Product listings (should)

- **Do:** Lay products in a ProductListing of ProductCards: the cards take the whole width of a row, the columns reflow, and the listing always shows whole rows (only the last row may be short, when there is nothing more). After the first rows put a "Show more" button (the default) or an endless scroll, chosen by setting. Keep the gap between cards of a dense listing at 4px on a phone
- **Don't:** lay product cards in a grid of your own, leave a row half full while more products are coming, or paginate by default: pagination is a setting and is not used unless it is asked for
- **Why:** A row with a hole at its end looks like a mistake, and a page number is a place people have to remember
- **Source:** Marketplace patterns: listing, load more; found by a script (`overgear/tools/check/spacing.mjs`)
- **Requests that lead here:** listing of products; catalog of items; show more; infinite scroll; pagination in the listing

### OG3 Catalog and listing are two things (should)

- **Do:** Keep the catalog and the listings apart, in the routes and in the code. The catalog is a classifier of the pages (groups, games, kinds of offers): it has no products and leads to a listing. A listing is the shop: filters, sort and the products in a ProductListing. The Catalog button of the header and of the bottom bar opens the catalog; a row of the catalog opens a listing
- **Don't:** open a listing from the Catalog button, or put products on a page of the catalog, or make a listing out of the classifier of the pages
- **Why:** A person who browses and a person who buys need different pages, and a catalog that is a listing hides the structure of the shop
- **Source:** E-commerce navigation: category pages and product listing pages; needs a review
- **Requests that lead here:** catalog page; classifier of the pages; category page; open the catalog
