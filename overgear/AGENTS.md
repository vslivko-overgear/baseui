# Instructions for Overgear

This directory is the Overgear sublibrary, showcase and store. Read the root `AGENTS.md`, the shared `CONSTITUTION.md`, then `overgear/CONSTITUTION.md` before changing an Overgear interface.

- All new rules specific to this store belong in `overgear/constitution/rules.js`, including navigation, catalog, checkout, empty states, merchandising and brand choices. This also applies when the request arrives at the repository root
- Use a new stable `OG`-prefixed id; do not reuse or renumber existing ids. Keep `do`, `dont`, `why`, `source`, `check` and `signals`; name a local tool when a rule is checked by a script
- Keep product-specific checks and tests under `overgear/tools/` and `overgear/tests/`. General accessibility, tokens and reusable component behavior belong to baseui; do not copy its rules here
- Do not put a store rule in `src/constitution/rules.js`, shared `AGENT_DEFAULTS`, shared component usage guidance or shared audit tools. Dependencies go from Overgear to baseui, never the other way around
- Follow the shared violation protocol, using `ConstitutionBanner` from `overgear/index.jsx` in repository sources (or `baseui-kit/overgear` in a consuming app). It understands shared ids and the local OG ids
- Edit rule sources, then run `npm run constitution` and `npm run registry`. `overgear/CONSTITUTION.md`, `overgear/AGENTS.md` and `overgear/CLAUDE.md` are generated by `overgear/tools/build/constitution.mjs`; change this generator to update these authoring instructions
- Run `npx vitest run overgear` for the product checks, and `npm test` for everything; the boundary of baseui is tested in `tests/boundary.test.js`

## Verifying a change

- `npm run verify` picks the checks (see the root `AGENTS.md`): a page of the example (`overgear/example/*.jsx`, `example.css`) is T1, about 20 seconds and a look at 1440 and 390 px; a shared part (`overgear/components`, `ui.jsx`, `order.jsx`, `shell.jsx`, `store.jsx`, the `*-core.js` files, `audience.js`, `theme.js`, the rules) is T2
- A decision a check or a rule holds is changed together with it (retire the id, write the new one); `overgear/tests/decisions.test.mjs` holds the decisions that are cheap to hold by reading the source

## The components of the sublibrary

- `overgear/components/` is the component library of Overgear on top of baseui: what every product of the store draws the same way, exported from `overgear/index.jsx`, with its styles in `overgear/components/components.css` (loaded by `overgear.css`). It depends on baseui only, never on the example
- Now: `CashbackTag` and `CashbackMark` (a tag of the bonus explains itself and ends with the mark of the coins), `PayBar` and `PayBarContent` (a pinned bar measures its height and keeps that room at the bottom of the page by itself), `money`
- A part moves here when a check keeps holding it across pages: the rule goes into the component, `overgear/tests/components.test.jsx` holds it, the browser check goes. The pages import the part and never restyle it

## The gaps between blocks

- One scale, the relation scale of baseui (`--bui-rel-*`), by how closely the blocks belong together: `pair` 0 (a title and the line that describes it), `hair` 4 (inside an element), `tight` 8 (a heading and its content), `related` 16 (the cards of a stack, and the default of `.og-stack`), `group` 24, `section` 40 (the big blocks of a page and the groups of a description). `.og-stack--xl` and `--2xl` are `section`, `--lg` is `group`, `--close` and `--snug` are `tight`
- A page names the relation (`og-stack`, `og-stack--group`, `og-stack--xl`) and has no gap of its own: no `gap` in a page rule, no number in a `style`. When a person asks for more air between big blocks, change the token or the class that all the pages share, not one page; when the same gap of a page is changed a third time, it is a relation that is missing, add it to the scale
- A list of cards (the orders, the payment history, the cart) is `og-stack` (16, `related`); the cards inside a card are `tight` or `hair`. `overgear/tests/decisions.test.mjs` holds that the stack classes are made of the relations

## Page density

- How far the blocks of a page are apart is `Page density`, not a gap of the page's own: `dense` (16px) for a list page such as the orders, nothing for most pages (a section, 40px), `airy` (56px) for a page of a few big blocks such as the wallet. The blocks are direct children of `Page`

## The games of the home page

- On a desktop the games are blocks of two rows of tiles of different widths, fitted into the width with no scroll: `fitTiles` in `overgear/audience.js` (pure, tested in `overgear/tests/catalog.test.mjs`) returns the blocks, the page grows the tiles by their widths. Both rows of a block end at the same place; an audience with a family (World of Warcraft) has it as a block of its own, the others are another block; when the tiles would be squeezed by more than a fifth, the last ones of the rest are left out, never the family. Do not turn it into a grid of equal columns
- `overgear/tools/check/tiles.mjs` measures it in a browser for every audience at three widths

## The order: the checkout, the cart and the mini-cart

- The parts that these three have in common are in `overgear/example/order.jsx` (`CartLine`, `ItemLines`, `OrderSummary`, `Promo`, `OrderHead`) and in the sublibrary (`PayBar`, `PayBarContent`). Change a line, the summary, the promo code row, the pay bar or the title there, once; never copy one into a surface or style it with a class of that surface
- The checkout (`cart.jsx`) is the latest base: when a surface differs from it in a shared part, the checkout is right. The cart page and the mini-cart pass their own labels and handlers (`label`, `action`, `onAction`, `pinned`), not their own markup
- "Add to cart" opens the mini-cart on a desktop and on a phone (`useAddedToCart`, the mini-cart is mounted in the shell); on a phone it is the standard `Dialog` (a sheet) with the pay bar as its footer. The mini-cart has the lines and the promo code card in its list, and the pay bar pinned at the bottom; it has no summary block
- `overgear/tools/check/order.mjs` compares the look of every shared part across the three on a desktop and on a phone, and fails when one of them drifts

## After the purchase: the orders

- The rules of the real service (og-frontend-4: orders listing, order page, payment result) are in one pure module, `overgear/example/order-core.js`, with its tests in `overgear/tests/orders.test.mjs`. Change a status, a text, a button, a lock or the way a payment ends there, and in the tests; the pages (`orders.jsx`, `order-page.jsx`, `order-result.jsx`) only draw it
- The orders a browser starts with are `order-seed.js`: a few of every status, so each state can be seen. The way an order moves on in the demo ("What happens next") is `nextEvents` and `applyEvent`
- The page /orders, /order/:link and /checkout/result/:result are the ones of the real service; a guest buyer becomes a temporary account (`store.temp`): it has the page of its order, not a history
- `overgear/tools/check/orders.mjs` plays the list, the page of an order in every status, the buyer's flows and the way from a payment, in a browser
